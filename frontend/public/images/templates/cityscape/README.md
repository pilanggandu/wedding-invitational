# Cityscape Urban Romance (Metropolitan Architecture) Template Assets

Collection of 10 modern architectural, metropolitan, and urban transit vector SVG assets created specifically for the **Cityscape Urban Romance (Metropolitan Architecture)** template (`cityscape`).

## Color Palette
- Metropolitan Sky Blue: `#2E8BC0`, `#1D78A0`, `#E8F4F8`
- Deep Navy & Architectural Concrete: `#0E384D`, `#1E293B`, `#64748B`
- Warm Sunset Glow: `#FEF08A`, `#F59E0B`
- Architectural Line White: `#FFFFFF`

## Asset Catalog

1. **`metropolitan-skyline-vector.svg`** (`480 × 160`): Layered modern skyscraper cityscape panorama with spire towers, glowing night windows, and waterfront reflections.
2. **`metro-transit-ticket.svg`** (`360 × 150`): Metropolitan subway transit boarding ticket with perforated notch, station stops, barcode, and tear-off stub.
3. **`suspension-bridge-arch.svg`** (`320 × 140`): Architectural suspension bridge cables, pylons, and road deck over water.
4. **`city-coordinates-pin.svg`** (`140 × 160`): Modern architectural map location pin with coordinate crosshairs and internal skyline silhouette.
5. **`blueprint-grid-motif.svg`** (`300 × 300`): Isometric architectural CAD blueprint grid pattern with building elevation line art.
6. **`vintage-streetlamp-glow.svg`** (`140 × 260`): European cast-iron streetlamp with warm radiating light halo bloom.
7. **`subway-metro-token.svg`** (`160 × 160`): Metallic brass subway token coin with couple monogram, train track rim, and embossed relief.
8. **`modern-compass-rose.svg`** (`180 × 180`): Minimalist navigational architectural compass rose with degree indicators.
9. **`skylight-glass-canopy.svg`** (`320 × 160`): Modernist geodesic glass atrium dome canopy with structural steel ribs.
10. **`city-skyline-postal-stamp.svg`** (`180 × 200`): Perforated postal stamp with skyscraper skyline artwork and wavy transit cancellation marks.
