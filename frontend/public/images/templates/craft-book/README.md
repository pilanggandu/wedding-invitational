# Craft Scrapbook Template Assets

Collection of 12 handcrafted vector SVG assets created specifically for the **Craft Scrapbook (Handcrafted Book View)** template (`craft-book`).

## Color Palette
- Kraft Base: `#6E5441`, `#8C7A6B`, `#DFD7CC`
- Terracotta Accent: `#C26D53`, `#E27F63`
- Dried Botanical Sage: `#79806B`, `#637050`
- Cream Paper & Foil: `#FAF7F2`, `#FAF5ED`, `#FFE484`

## Asset Catalog

1. **`craft-spiral-spine.svg`** (`80 × 500`): Realistic double-wire spiral binder rings with hole punches and drop shadows for the notebook spine.
2. **`washi-tape-terracotta.svg`** (`320 × 80`): Terracotta translucent washi tape with delicate botanical floral pattern and ripped deckle edges.
3. **`washi-tape-stripes.svg`** (`320 × 80`): Diagonal striped sage-green and kraft washi tape strip with torn serrated edges.
4. **`polaroid-kraft-frame.svg`** (`320 × 380`): Handcrafted polaroid photo mount with corner slit tabs, photo cutout window, and handwritten caption guide.
5. **`airmail-border-badge.svg`** (`300 × 140`): Par Avion classic chevron border, blue/red diagonal stripes, and vintage postal cancellation badge.
6. **`boarding-pass-ticket.svg`** (`380 × 160`): Perforated wedding travel boarding pass / train ticket with tear-off stub, flight notch, and barcode.
7. **`kraft-pocket-flap.svg`** (`320 × 180`): Folded kraft envelope V-flap with traditional string-tie washer clasp.
8. **`thumbtack-pin.svg`** (`120 × 120`): 3D wooden craft thumbtack pushpin with realistic drop shadow and metallic tip.
9. **`pressed-fern-sprig.svg`** (`160 × 280`): Botanical pressed dried fern frond specimen with organic dried sage leaf coloring.
10. **`craft-postage-stamp.svg`** (`180 × 220`): Perforated postage stamp featuring interlocking wedding rings and cancellation postmark.
11. **`stitched-seam-divider.svg`** (`500 × 30`): Needlecraft embroidery running thread stitch divider line with needle hole punctures.
12. **`kraft-wax-seal.svg`** (`160 × 160`): Terracotta wax seal with irregular organic rim and embossed heart emblem.
