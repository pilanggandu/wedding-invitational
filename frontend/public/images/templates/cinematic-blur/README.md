# Ethereal Dreamscape (Cinematic Motion Blur) Template Assets

Collection of 10 authentic 35mm film, anamorphic, and cinema-grade vector SVG assets created specifically for the **Ethereal Dreamscape (Cinematic Motion Blur)** template (`cinematic-blur`).

## Color Palette
- Cinematic Noir / Base: `#0F1116`, `#14161C`, `#1A1D24`
- Warm Silver & Champagne: `#DFD2BD`, `#C5B392`
- Recording & Lens Flare Highlights: `#EF4444`, `#F59E0B`, `#38BDF8`
- Film Negative Elements: `#FAF7F2`, `#FAF5EF`

## Asset Catalog

1. **`film-strip-35mm.svg`** (`100 × 500`): Authentic 35mm film strip with rectangular sprocket holes, frame exposure windows, frame numbers (01A-05A), and edge code marks.
2. **`cinemascope-crop-frame.svg`** (`480 × 200`): 2.39:1 widescreen letterbox matte overlay with focal corner crop marks, center crosshair, and aspect ratio labels.
3. **`film-slate-clapper.svg`** (`240 × 200`): Realistic director clapperboard with angled striped arm, production title, scene/take slots, and 24 FPS indicator.
4. **`anamorphic-lens-flare.svg`** (`500 × 140`): Widescreen anamorphic optical streak flare in cyan and golden amber with bright central starburst.
5. **`film-countdown-leader.svg`** (`220 × 220`): Vintage film projector countdown target with rotating wipe sector and central wedding symbol.
6. **`soundtrack-waveform.svg`** (`480 × 60`): Optical stereo soundwave audio groove for section headers and music dividers.
7. **`rec-viewfinder-hud.svg`** (`360 × 220`): High-end camera viewfinder HUD overlay with glowing RED REC dot, timecode, 4K 24FPS specs, and battery gauge.
8. **`vintage-light-leak.svg`** (`320 × 320`): Warm analog film light leak flare in radiant amber, tangerine, and gold.
9. **`camera-aperture-iris.svg`** (`200 × 200`): 9-blade cinema lens aperture diaphragm with metallic gradient blades.
10. **`cinema-laurel-wreath.svg`** (`240 × 160`): Cannes-style prestigious film festival golden laurel wreath with 35mm film reel emblem.
