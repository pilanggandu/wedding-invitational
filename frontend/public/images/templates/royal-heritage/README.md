# Royal Heritage (Classic Formal Emerald & Gold) Template Assets

Collection of 10 regal, baroque, and architectural vector SVG assets created specifically for the **Royal Heritage (Classic Formal Emerald & Gold)** template (`royal-heritage`).

## Color Palette
- Royal Zamrud Emerald Base: `#0F2D24`, `#081C16`, `#2D6A4F`
- Imperial Antique Gold: `#C8A97E`, `#E8D7B8`, `#FDF4DC`, `#8C6D3F`
- Classical Parchment: `#FAF8F0`, `#FBF8EE`

## Asset Catalog

1. **`royal-palace-archway.svg`** (`320 × 440`): Architectural palace dome gateway (kubah gapura istana) with Corinthian pillars, keystone crown, and arabesque filigree medallion.
2. **`imperial-crown-crest.svg`** (`220 × 220`): Royal regalia crown flanked by laurel wreath branches and jeweled accents with serif monogram center.
3. **`baroque-corner-filigree.svg`** (`180 × 180`): Classical 24K gold foil corner filigree flourish with scrolling acanthus leaves and royal rosettes.
4. **`emerald-wax-seal.svg`** (`160 × 160`): Deep imperial emerald wax seal with gold-embossed royal crown, laurel wreath, and initial seal.
5. **`damask-filigree-divider.svg`** (`480 × 50`): Symmetrical European royal damask divider with central fleuron palmette and scrolling gold tendrils.
6. **`gilded-border-frame.svg`** (`360 × 480`): Classical double-beveled gold formal certificate border with ornamental corner rosettes.
7. **`royal-candelabra-crest.svg`** (`200 × 200`): Antique palace 3-branch candelabra chandelier vignette with warm golden glowing flames.
8. **`heraldic-monogram-shield.svg`** (`200 × 240`): Medieval noble knight escutcheon shield with royal crown, gold chevron, and entwined letters.
9. **`golden-scroll-ribbon.svg`** (`320 × 90`): Flowing heraldic gold banner ribbon with swallowtail ends and classical serif title.
10. **`classic-fleur-de-lis.svg`** (`120 × 140`): Refined French imperial fleur-de-lis royal emblem with engraved multi-stop gold shading.
