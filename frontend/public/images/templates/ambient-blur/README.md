# Ambient Chroma (Adaptive Photo Atmosphere) Template Assets

Collection of 10 modern optical, chromatic, and glassmorphic vector SVG assets created specifically for the **Ambient Chroma (Adaptive Photo Atmosphere)** template (`ambient-blur`).

## Color Palette
- Midnight Obsidian Base: `#0A0D14`, `#11141E`
- Luminous Coral / Terracotta: `#E07A5F`, `#F4A261`
- Prismatic Spectral Accent: `#38BDF8`, `#A855F7`, `#EC4899`, `#FCD34D`
- Frosted Specular Glass: `#FFFFFF` (multi-tier opacities)

## Asset Catalog

1. **`chromatic-prism-flare.svg`** (`400 × 200`): Optical dispersion prism beam with spectral rainbow dispersion, anamorphic glare, and core reflection.
2. **`frosted-glass-card-overlay.svg`** (`360 × 480`): Frosted glass card panel with specular highlight rim, rounded corners, and corner alignment ticks.
3. **`aurora-glow-halo.svg`** (`320 × 320`): Multi-stop radiant chromatic ambient glow aura for photo backdrops and ambient light fields.
4. **`modern-viewfinder-crosshairs.svg`** (`320 × 320`): High-fashion editorial photography viewfinder brackets, rule-of-thirds markers, and center focal crosshair.
5. **`minimal-monogram-ring.svg`** (`200 × 200`): Contemporary circular typography monogram with dual gradient rings and hairline letterforms.
6. **`glass-crystal-sparkle.svg`** (`120 × 120`): 4-point optical crystal star sparkle with radial dispersion bloom.
7. **`editorial-barcode-tag.svg`** (`280 × 90`): Luxury fashion-editorial registry badge with vertical barcode, geographic coordinates, and clean typography.
8. **`fluid-wave-divider.svg`** (`480 × 40`): Continuous smooth sine-wave chromatic divider line with anchor orbital point.
9. **`iridescent-badge.svg`** (`160 × 160`): Beveled octagon badge with holographic gradient rim and ampersand typographic center.
10. **`bokeh-aperture-overlay.svg`** (`240 × 240`): Photographic 7-blade camera iris diaphragm graphic with translucent polygonal blades.
